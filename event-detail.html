Startup Bootcamp

← Back to Events / Event Details / Startup Bootcamp

📝 Event Description

Join us for a comprehensive full-day bootcamp where you'll learn how to validate business ideas, create compelling pitch decks, and present to real startup mentors. This intensive program is designed for students interested in innovation and entrepreneurship.

Whether you're a complete beginner or have some experience with startups, this bootcamp will provide you with practical tools and insights to transform your ideas into viable business ventures.

You'll network with like-minded students, receive feedback from industry professionals, and leave with a clear roadmap for your entrepreneurial journey.
